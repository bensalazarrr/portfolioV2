## Why

A job hunt produces its own paperwork: applications tracked in notes, quotes in a spreadsheet, a resume that exists in six versions. Orbit puts those in one place instead of scattering them across apps.

## Scope

Thirteen modules across four areas — personal (projects, tasks, goals, learning, journal), profession (job history, applications, resume generator), freelance (clients, quotes, invoices, payments), and an assistant. Dark and dense by design.

## State

In development. The workspace shell and module routing work; the resume generator and the invoice flow are the parts still being built.
