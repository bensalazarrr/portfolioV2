## The brief

A research group needed their findings to exist as something a reader could use, not a PDF. The hub had to explain cloud-based accounting to students and small-business owners without assuming an accounting background.

## Structure

Four sections — overview, financial reporting quality, regulatory compliance, and a digital handbook — plus sample figures and user voices. Sample data is labelled as sample data throughout, which matters when the audience is still learning what they are looking at.

## Design

Dark and high-contrast, built around a large typographic hero with the sample dashboard treated as the focal object rather than decoration.
