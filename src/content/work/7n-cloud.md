## Purpose

Most explanations of cloud accounting stop at the diagram. This is the diagram made usable: sign in as a sample service business, record transactions, switch roles, and watch the ledger update as you work.

## What it demonstrates

Double-entry posting, a ledger that reconciles, and separation between the people who record transactions and the people who approve accounts. Sign-up exists but stays in a queue until an account is approved.

## Where it sits

Built alongside research on cloud-based accounting systems, and used as the working example that the written material refers to.
