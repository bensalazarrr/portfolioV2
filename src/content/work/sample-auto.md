## What it is

A concept build for a workshop that does not exist, used to work a full commercial site end to end: services with PHP estimates, a parts catalogue with search, category filters and sorting, booking and inquiry forms, and a complete dark and light design system.

## Detail work

Forms run on React Hook Form with Zod validation, including loading and success states. Product dialogs are keyboard-accessible. Motion is used for entrances only, and reduced motion is respected throughout.

## Stack

Next.js App Router, TypeScript, Tailwind CSS on a custom semantic token system, Motion for React, and Lucide icons.
