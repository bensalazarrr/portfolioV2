## What it is

Four lessons, three ways to play, and a local best score for each mode. It runs by opening a file in a browser, which is the point — a school or a small organisation can host it anywhere without a toolchain.

## Accessibility

Keyboard-operable answers, visible focus, reduced-motion support, native dialogs, and separate light and dark themes with distinct handling for image-heavy screens.

## Content

Character material is original, with references and credits retained. Seventeen basic characters, guided lessons, and untimed practice alongside a ten-second challenge mode.
